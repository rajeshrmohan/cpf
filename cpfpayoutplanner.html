What is an annuity?

An annuity is a series of equal payments that uses up a sum of money over a fixed period. Each month you withdraw a little, and the balance you haven't touched yet keeps earning interest. That interest is why you receive more in total than you started with. This planner builds that annuity from your own OA. Unlike CPF LIFE, it stops when the money runs out, and anything left belongs to your nominees.

How CPF rules shape these numbers

  • Interest is paid on your lowest balance each month and credited once a year on 31 Dec, then compounded. Your monthly withdrawal happens before the month's lowest balance is taken, so this planner uses the same method. Planners that compound monthly show slightly higher payouts.
  • OA earns 2.5% p.a. The rate is pegged to local bank rates but cannot fall below 2.5% by law. It has stayed at the floor since 1999.
  • Extra interest does not land in your OA. From 55, the extra 1–2% on your first S$60,000 (up to S$20,000 from OA) goes into your Retirement Account, so it isn't counted here.
  • At 55 your Retirement Account is formed first. Your SA closes; SA savings then OA savings move to your RA up to your Full Retirement Sum. SA savings above that go to your OA. What stays in your OA can be withdrawn any time from 55.
  • You can always withdraw at least S$5,000 from 55, even if you haven't set aside your retirement sum.
  • CPF LIFE is separate. It pays from your RA for life, starting any age from 65 to 70. This planner covers only the OA savings you can freely withdraw.

How these CPF LIFE figures work

  • 2026 figures are CPF Board's estimates for the Standard Plan from 65: S$950 (BRS), S$1,780 (FRS) and S$3,440 (ERS), assuming 4% interest on RA savings.
  • Retirement sums from 2016 to 2027 are the official figures. Payouts for years other than 2026 are scaled from the 2026 estimates in line with each year's sums. MOM's own 2027 BRS estimate is close to S$1,000, which matches.
  • Years after 2027 are projections. Those sums haven't been announced, so they grow 3.5% a year from 2027, the rate used from 2023 to 2027.
  • Before 2025 the ERS was 1.5 times the FRS; from 2025 it is 2 times.
  • Deferring raises payouts by up to 7% for each year after 65, up to 35% more if you start at 70. Your RA keeps earning interest meanwhile.
  • Actual payouts vary. Women receive slightly less because of longer life expectancy, and the Basic and Escalating plans pay differently. Use CPF's CPF LIFE Estimator for your own figure.
